## 🔥 About Us

> **RevivifAI builds and restores frontier language and multimodal models into tools that *actually* help humans, and not substitute them.**

We engineer techniques that let models **perceive** and **know what they don't know** — eliminating hallucination risks through calibrated uncertainty. Every model we publish is designed to be:

<br>

<div align="center">

| 🧠 **Capable** | 🤝 **Honest** | 🔍 **Legible** |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| *Trained to reason before answering* | *Flags its own uncertainty* | *Stays transparent to the prompt holder* |

</div>

---

## 🌟 Our Philosophy

> We seek to create hyperintelligent general intellects that aid humanity and augment people, such that both have a continued purpose to improve each other.

Intelligence, human or artificial, is at its best when it is answerable to a partner. A model that only reflects its creator's fears becomes sycophantic; one that only mirrors its user's wishes becomes dangerous.

### We build for the space in between:

<div align="center">

| ✨ **Calibration with Courage** | ⚡ **Capability with Character** |
|:---|:---|
| Models that know what they don't know | Power tempered by honesty |

</div>
